# About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer in the Thermal and Structural Materials group within the Applied Materials Division at [Argonne National Laboratory](https://www.anl.gov/). My work focuses on predictive, physics-based modeling of material behavior in harsh environments, with an emphasis on materials produced by advanced manufacturing processes. Prior to my current role, I was a Postdoctoral Appointee in the same group, where I studied creep deformation in diffusion-bonded and laser powder bed fusion microstructures.

I earned my PhD in [Mechanical Engineering](https://mane.rpi.edu/) from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://www.rpi.edu/), where I worked on computational modeling of microstructure evolution of Ti-6Al-4V during thermomechanical processing. I also hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the [University at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu/), State University of New York, and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from [Vellore Institute of Technology](https://vit.ac.in/).

My fields of interest include Computational Solid Mechanics, Crystal Plasticity, Finite Element Methods, and High Performance Computing. Outside of work, I enjoy running and painting.
