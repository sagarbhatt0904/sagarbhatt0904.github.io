# About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer at [Argonne National Laboratory](https://www.anl.gov/), where I work in the Applied Materials Division on predictive modeling of material behavior in harsh environments using physics-based models. Before this role, I was a Postdoctoral Appointee at Argonne in the Thermal and Structural Materials Modeling and Simulations Group, modeling creep deformation in diffusion-bonded and additively manufactured microstructures.

I received my PhD in [Mechanical Engineering](https://mane.rpi.edu/) from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://www.rpi.edu/), where I worked under the supervision of Prof. [Antoinette Maniatty](https://homepages.rpi.edu/~maniaa/) on computational modeling of microstructure evolution of Ti-6Al-4V under thermo-mechanical loading using finite element crystal plasticity and Monte Carlo methods to simulate deformation and grain growth, respectively. I received my MS from [University at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu/), The State University of New York and B.Tech from [Vellore Institute of Technology](https://vit.ac.in/), both in Mechanical Engineering as well.

My fields of interest include Computational Solid Mechanics, Crystal Plasticity, Finite Element Methods, and High Performance Computing.
